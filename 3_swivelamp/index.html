Swivel Desk Lamp

Overview

This project involves reverse-engineering a plastic kitchen cheese grater. The product was fully dissassembled, measured, and reconstructed as an 8-part 3D assembly to reflect the original product's geometry, fit, and function.

Objective

Completed as a term project, the objetive was to develop hands-on experience in reconstruction of a real-world product while understanding component interaction, design intent, and manufacturing considerations.

Skills Used

  • Precise measurement fulfilling assembly constraints
  • Advanced
  • Assembly mates & exploded view animation
  • Technical drawings & dimensioning

Tools & Software

  • SolidWorks
  • Vernier Calipers
  • Micrometer Gauge

Obstacles & Solutions

Obstacle:

Obtaining accurate measurements due to the enclosed / minimal design of the product.

Solution:

Averaged multiple measurement samples for each feature to reduce human and instrument error, resulting in more reliable dimensions.

Obstacle:

Several features were located on curved or cylindrical surfaces.

Solution:

Offset reference planes (and tangent planes) were created to accurately define sketch locations and feature orientations.

Obstacle:

Establishing core geometry for complex part.

Solution:

Applied advanced modeling techniques like patterned extrusuions, swept profiles, thin body, etc to closely replicate real-word details.

Obstacle:

Assembly fit constraints.

Solution:

Dimensions were adjusted through multiple iterations and advanced mates such as gear, width, and angle limits were applied to ensure correct motion and alignment.
